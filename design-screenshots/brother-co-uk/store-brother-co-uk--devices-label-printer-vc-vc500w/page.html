Product Overview

The compact VC-500W wireless colour photo printer features ZINK® Zero Ink Technology that allows you to print amazing full colour labels, photos, stickers and more up to 420mm long in a single pass without the need for ink cartridges, ribbons or toners. The VC-500W makes it fun and easy to decorate, personalize and create handmade gifts, gift wrap, greeting cards, invitations, photo projects, party favours, stickers and family fun items to tackling a myriad of organization tasks, business projects, address labels, scrapbooking, home decor, and more.

All colours required for high-quality photo printing are embedded in the ZINK® paper which has an adhesive backing that adheres to most surfaces. The wireless interface offers easy connectivity – and lets you print from virtually anywhere or share on a network. Create and print from your mobile device when connected to a wireless network. The FREE Color Label Editor app includes thousands of personalized features, such as frames, fonts, art, backgrounds and ready-made designs for your mobile device (download from app stores).

Your designs, your way

Peel back the backing and stick your designs wherever you want, or keep them as regular printouts to share as gifts or add to a photo frame or wall – it’s up to you. And, with continuous roll printing, there are no fixed sizes – choose whatever dimensions you like.

Full colour printing without ink

Print your favourite photos in full colour with ease. Using our ZINK® colour paper and Zero Ink® technology, your photos will never smudge and they’ll be water and tear resistant, so your memories will never fade.

Print on the go

Print photos from your phone or tablet wherever you are, whenever you want. Our free Color Label Editor 2 app lets you pull photos direct from your camera roll or social media - letting you turn that selfie into a hard copy in seconds.
 

Customise and personalise

As part of the free Colour Label Editor 2 app, you can take your photos and edit them in real time. Add captions, emojis and filters on the fly and give that personal touch to your photos before getting your hands on them.

Dynamic printing

Print photos and labels in different sizes using our range of five-metre-long rolls, designed to save you reloading.
