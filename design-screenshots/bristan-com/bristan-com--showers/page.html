Whether you need a little help waking up in the morning or want to feel reinvigorated after a long day at work,
our range of mixer showers are guaranteed to leave you feeling refreshed.

From our exposed mini valve showers and stylish bar showers to our trend led concealed showers,
we have a variety of styles and finishes to suit any bathroom.

Shower Packs

A smart showering solution that makes specification straightforward and installation efficient.

The integral two outlet diverter allows effortless switching between the drench head and handset, or operation of each individually, offering smooth control, dependable temperature regulation, and a luxurious drench.

With quality materials, a clean modern design, and practical details for installers and homeowners alike, this pack brings reliable functionality and elegant style together without compromise. For complete peace of mind, this all in one box - pack includes shower valve with integral two outlet diverter slimline 200mm round drench head with shower arm6 in a single, ready-to-fit shower kit.
View the range  
Mini Valve Showers

Our mini valve shower collections are easy to install and perfect to retrofit onto existing pipework with adjustable elbow inlets to suit 110mm - 155mm centers, so there’s no need for retiling! Supplied as a complete kit, you have everything you need all in one box, so nothing to buy separately.

Our Sonique, Acute and Capri are available in a classic Chrome finish while Onza is available now in both a Chrome or Black finish.
Find out more  


Bar Showers

Bar showers have been a part of bathrooms for years and it’s easy to see why. This type of mixer shower is compact, versatile and easy to access for maintenance as it sits on top of tiles.

Most of ranges feature our bar shower fast-fixing kit, Wallmount 12, with integrated front-facing isolation valves making it easy to fit, maintain and replace. So there’s no need to disturb existing pipework or tiles.

Available in a wide range of compatible shower styles for design flexibility, and supplied as a complete kit, with nothing to buy separately.
Find out more

Molida Recessed Shower

Introducing the European inspired Molida range. Featuring a diamond knurling pattern in the peg lever handles to add a touch of depth and elegance. 

Available in three distinct finishes, these will blend effortlessly with a wide range of bathrooms, cloakroom, and ensuites.
