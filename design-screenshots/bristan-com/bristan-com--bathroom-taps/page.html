One hole mixer taps
___________________

A one-tap hole mixer combines hot and cold water in a single spout. They are controlled by one lever handle to adjust temperature and flow. Available in as basin mixer taps and bath filler tap options, a range of sizes and on-trend designs, they're ideal for both style and practicality.

Two hole taps
___________________

Two-tap-hole basin taps and bath taps feature separate controls for hot and cold water, for a classic look and reliable performance. Perfect for traditional bathrooms, they combine timeless elegance with Bristan quality.

Wall Mounted Taps
___________________

A wall mounted tap is installed directly into the wall, saving surface space on your basin or bathtub. Available in both basin mixer and bath options, they create a clean, minimalist look and are ideal for modern spaces. For wash areas, pair wall mounted bath taps with complementary basin taps for a seamless finish.

Eco Start

Eco Start technology lets you choose when hot water is required, saving money on your energy bill, and reducing your carbon footprint. Double win! Available on our Altum, Appeal, Glid, Hourglass, Molida, Saffron and Tangram Basin Mixer Taps.

The handle is set with cold water as the default start position. Hot water is only activated when the tap is moved from the central position (towards the red VBL marking). 

The opening of the tap in the cold position stops unnecessary engagement of the boiler, saving energy, money and making your home more eco-friendly.

The Perfect Finish


Whether your bathroom is craving chrome, or seeking a statement piece, you’re sure to find the perfect finish with Bristan bathroom taps. Available in tried and tested chrome, the ever-popular black, striking brushed brass and sophisticated gun metal grey. 

Chrome


A true classic finish. Chrome creates a contract to darker colour schemes while effortlessly blending with lighter spaces. Unassuming, the addition of chrome will tie the whole room together and pairs beautifully with bath taps or a Bath Filler for cohesive style. Coordinate chrome basin taps with matching bathroom sink taps for a unified look.

Black


Black is a deep, elegant colour with a matte finish that offers a striking contrast against lighter surfaces. Perfect for creating a modern and luxurious look, black bathroom taps add a sense of understated sophistication, whether you choose wall mounted bath taps or a Bath Shower Mixer Tap for added versatility. Pair with minimalist basin taps to complete the scheme.


Brushed Brass


Brushed Brass is a standout finish. Its subtle yet striking look adds a touch of luxury to any room. Complementing a variety of colour schemes, Brushed Brass bathroom taps make a timeless and versatile choice for any space and style, from Basin Mixer Taps to coordinated bath taps and bathroom sink taps.


Gun Metal Grey


Our gunmetal taps and showers in a dark, sophisticated grey colour with a subtle lustre. Its inspiration comes from historic colours and is perfect for a timeless feel to your bathroom or kitchen. Complete the look with a Bath Filler or wall mounted bath taps to maximise space and style, and finish with complementary basin taps.
