Specifying for Affordable Housing



Understanding the challenges faced by affordable housing specifiers, Bristan’s products deliver on functionality, safety and style, while providing value for money without compromising on quality. The Club bathroom taps and the Design Utility taps, for example, combine practical design, affordability and quality whilst also benefiting from a comprehensive product warranty.

In the affordable housing market in particular, taps and showers are often used by people with different abilities, and so Bristan offers products that are safe and easy to grip and use, without compromising on style or aesthetics.

For example, the Bristan Lever tap is a modern, practical tap that is easy for people of all abilities to use.  Meanwhile, the Design Utility mixer shower is TMV2 accredited, providing additional hot water safety for the householder and peace of mind for the specifier. Bristan also offer bath shower mixers that are thermostatic and Part G compliant.

In the kitchen Bristan also offer kitchen taps and sinks. Our pillar, monobloc and twin mixer taps for affordable homes, new build and refurbishment are durable and practical without sacrificing the looks that are so important to creating a home environment.

QUALITY AND PERFORMANCE - In House Testing and Accreditation

Bristan has a state-of-the-art in-house test laboratory from its HQ in Tamworth, Staffordshire to ensure all products continue to meet guideline and legal requirements as well as third-party certifications. For example,

Bristan was first in industry to have a UKAS 17025 facility, which can test products for submission to WRAS to gain product certification, demonstrating Bristan commitment to customer safety.

With over £1.6 million invested in the test laboratory in recent years it means Bristan showers and taps are not just fully compliant but boasts the absolute highest level of product excellence, helping ensure you meet your own organisation KPI’s.

FAMILY SAFETY - Part G Regs and TMV's

All of our social housing products are designed for ease of use by all ages and abilities. Many have simple, easy sequential controls that make handwashing child's play.

Our showers for social housing feature built-in thermostatic temperature controls that keep water at a constant temperature, regardless of whether another tap is turned on elsewhere in the house. Not just more comfortable, but safer too, as the risk of scalding is reduced.

Download our Water Safety leaflet for more information.

RESIDENT SATISFACTION - Reduce Water Bills

Your homes also need to stand up to the latest legislative requirements when it comes to water efficiency. Especially when larger housing association developments or local authority estates can be made up of hundreds of individual homes in which each person can consume, on average, 150 litres of water per day*.

So as well as fitting limiting technology to our products, we've chosen our social housing range, in part, for it's water efficiency. Where appropriate, you'll see flow rates on many of our data sheets indicating just how much water they use under the European Water Labelling scheme.

Bristan also work with end-users to follow trends and ensure that our taps and showers operate as they should. For example Orta offers a stylish design but is fit for all age groups. Our extensive range of kitchen taps include options with long-lever and easy to use controls.

*Consumer Council for Water Figures

WARRANTY AND MAINTENANCE - Ease of Spares and Product Testing

At Bristan we offer a 10 year guarantee on our kitchen and bathroom taps for social housing applications whilst our domestic mixer showers come with a 5 guarantee. When you turn to Bristan you can be confident that you have chosen a reliable, safe shower or tap to help your customer in their home.

In the event of a problem Bristan has a national in-house field service team who are available to help if a problem does occur so a fast resolution.

From our head office in Tamworth, Staffordshire we carry a huge range of spare parts available next day to help on products when needed and ensure your customer has a fully working product as quickly as possible.

SUSTAINABILITY - Every Drop Counts

Our 'every drop counts' approach to water efficiency is reflected in the number of products incorporating flow limiting features and our support for the European Water Label Scheme. As well as helping you meet Part G of the Building Regulations, that's a big plus for environment savvy and cost-conscious social housing providers too.


We have also achieved the highly-regarded Carbon Trust Standard certification following working on reducing our energy. To read more click here.

We also operate an ethical supply chain to limit consumption of non-renewable and reduce the environmental impact of our delivery vehicles.

To further supports our commitment to sustainability, we are also active members of the Supply Chain Sustainability School.
