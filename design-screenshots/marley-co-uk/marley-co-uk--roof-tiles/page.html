Roof tiles

The Marley range of roof tiles have been designed for performance, aesthetics and easy installation. Choose from clay tiles and concrete tiles in a wide range of colours and styles, as well as the Marley SolarTile® solar panel and cedar shingles and shakes.

Products are available to suit any roof pitch, including very low pitches, and all roofing tiles are compatible with the Marley Roof System, making specification a breeze.

Roof tiles to suit any project

From traditional to modern, handmade to precision-made, the Marley range of roof tiles offers unlimited design versatility, coupled with excellent performance.

Our collection of clay roof tiles and concrete roof tiles includes pantiles, flat and interlocking designs and products that mimic natural slate. Choose from colours ranging from reds and browns to various shades of grey. We’ve designed our roof tiles for easy installation and dry fixing, and products are available for pitches as low as 12.5°.

The innovative SolarTile® is a roof-integrated solar panel that provides both a roof covering and electricity generation. It is suitable for both refurbishment and new-build projects and can be installed individually or in an array.

Our beautiful Western Red Cedar shingles and shakes are a beautiful and sustainable alternative to clay and concrete roofing tiles. They provide a natural and unique appearance that can suit both domestic and commercial applications.

Explore our range of roof tiles

With more than 80 different roof tiles to choose from, Marley’s range offers something for every project, whether you’re refurbishing a heritage roof or constructing a new-build housing development.

A complete roofing system

Our extensive range of roof tiles is fully compatible with the rest of the Marley Roof System, giving you a single source for all your roofing needs.

From battens and underlays to roof tiles, solar panels, fire barriers, vents, and dry-fix fittings, we can provide everything to construct a reliable and beautiful roof that’s also easy to install and specify.

What's more, a complete Marley Roof System comes with a 15-year guarantee for further peace of mind.

Want to know more?

Whether you have a question about choosing roof tiles or need specification support, our dedicated team of experts is on hand to help. Contact us for more information or download our latest roofing products guide.
